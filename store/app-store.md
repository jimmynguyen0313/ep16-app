## Description (4000)

Ep.16 is a diary for every K-drama you've watched, are watching, or can't wait to start.

LOG WHAT YOU WATCH
Add any K-drama in a couple of taps. Mark it Watched, Watching or Want to watch, rate it with half stars, write down the scene that wrecked you, and pick the day you finished. Track which episode you're on while you're mid-binge.

REWATCH YOUR FAVORITES
Watching Crash Landing on You for the third time? Log every rewatch with its own date, rating and note, and see how your feelings changed.

FOLLOW YOUR FAVORITE ACTORS
Open any actor to see their photo, biography and every K-drama they've starred in, newest first. Heart the ones you love and they're always one tap away in your diary.

WRAPPED
Look back on your year: dramas finished, episodes watched, rewatches, your average rating, your busiest month, top genres, highest rated dramas, and the actors who kept showing up.

FOR YOU
Get suggestions based on the dramas you rated highest, with a note saying which drama each one came from.

BROWSE AND SEARCH
Explore popular K-dramas by genre, or search by English or Korean title. Every drama page shows the cast, the characters they play, the network, episode count and synopsis.

YOUR DIARY, ON EVERY DEVICE
Sign in with your email and your diary syncs. No ads, no tracking. Delete your account and everything in it whenever you like.

Drama, cast and image data come from The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Create a demo account in the app before submitting (for example `appreview@` an address you control, with a password you'll share only here) and enter it in **Sign-in information**. ## Screenshots

Apple needs screenshots for one size: the **6.9-inch iPhone** set (1320 × 2868 or 1290 × 2796 pixels, portrait). Up to 10; 3 to 5 is plenty. Apple scales them down for smaller iPhones. The app is iPhone-only, so no iPad screenshots are needed.

Take them from a TestFlight build on your own phone (side button + volume up), signed in to the demo account:

1. **Browse:** the poster grid with For you at the top.
2. **Drama page:** a well-known drama with its backdrop, In your diary, and a rewatch.

4. **Diary:** stats, the Wrapped card, favorite actors and a few rows.

If your iPhone isn't a 6.9-inch model (iPhone 16 Pro Max, 16 Plus, 15 Pro Max, 15 Plus and similar), its screenshots will be the wrong size for that set. Apple also accepts the 6.5-inch set (1284 × 2778 or 1242 × 2688), or the screenshots can be placed on a background at the right size.
